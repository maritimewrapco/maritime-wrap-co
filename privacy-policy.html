Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Maritime Wrap Co. ("we," "us," or "our") operates maritimewrapco.com. This page explains what information we collect when you visit or use this site, and how it's used.

Information You Provide

When you submit a quote request through our form, we collect the information you enter, which may include:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • City/marina location
  • Boat make, model, length, and year
  • Details about the service you're interested in and your project
  • Photos of your boat, if you choose to upload them

This information is used solely to respond to your quote request and communicate with you about our services.

How Quote Requests Are Processed

Quote form submissions are delivered to us by FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service. We do not store submitted form data in a database on our own servers; it is emailed directly to us.

Analytics and Advertising

We use Google Ads to measure the effectiveness of our advertising. When you submit a quote request, our site shares the following with Google in a privacy-safe (hashed) format so that Google can match your submission to the ad that brought you to our site:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Name

This data is hashed (encrypted) before it's sent and is used only for ad measurement and conversion tracking — it is not sold or shared for any other purpose. You can learn more about how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/privacy, and about Google Ads Enhanced Conversions specifically at support.google.com/google-ads.

Cookies

Google Ads may set cookies in your browser to help measure ad performance. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Data Retention

We retain quote request information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for reasonable record-keeping afterward. You may request that we delete your information at any time by contacting us below.

Data Security

Maritime Wrap Co. is based in Stuart, Florida, and this policy is governed by Florida law. In line with the Florida Information Protection Act (FIPA, Fla. Stat. § 501.171), we use reasonable security measures appropriate for a small business to protect the personal information we handle. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals without unreasonable delay and no later than 30 days after discovering the breach, as required by Florida law.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.
