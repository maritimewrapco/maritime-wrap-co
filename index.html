About Maritime Wrap Co.

Marine wraps done right.

Maritime Wrap Co. is the trade name under which Riley Langford operates Maritime Wrap LLC.

Before founding the company, Riley spent years in the marine wrap trade working as an independent contractor, wrapping vessels on the water and in the shop. The projects shown in this portfolio from that period were performed personally by Riley Langford as an individual — not by Maritime Wrap Co. or Maritime Wrap LLC — and are presented here as examples of his individual craftsmanship and experience prior to founding the company.

Maritime Wrap Co. is the next step: the same hands, the same standards, now under one roof. Every project taken on by the company receives careful surface preparation, precise installation, and an owner directly involved in the outcome.

Our Process

1Consultation

We walk you through material, finish and coverage options and scope the job to your vessel and timeline.

2Design & Proof

Your layout is custom-built to the boat's exact measurements and sent over for approval before production.

3Surface Prep & Install

The hull is cleaned, decontaminated and prepped, then the wrap is applied and hand-detailed panel by panel.

4Final Quality Check

Every seam, edge and panel is inspected before we hand the boat back to you.
