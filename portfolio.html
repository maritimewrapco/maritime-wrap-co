About Us

Maritime Wrap Co. is a Florida-based boat wrap company specializing in vinyl wraps and paint protection film (PPF) for private yachts, charter fleets and commercial clients. We give owners a fresh look and long-lasting protection for less than the cost and downtime of a repaint.

We have worked on everything from center consoles and sport fish to motor yachts and superyachts, including on-site wrap work on charter catamarans in the British Virgin Islands. We travel to your yard and cover our own travel and lodging, so partnering with us costs the marina nothing.

Projects shown from before the company was founded were performed by Riley Langford as an individual contractor, not by Maritime Wrap Co.
